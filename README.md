## Why this excuse

The judge weighs, in order: an excuse a dog gives after being caught eating the homework, in the dog's own voice; the bigger laugh, with a twist or a piece of dog logic; sounding like a real dog with a dog's priorities; working on its own; and feeling fresh. The line is dog logic doing the whole job: possession by placement (it was on MY bed), evidence by smell (faintly of chicken), and a legal system invented on the spot (nobody signed it, so by dog law it was mine). The dog believes it has done nothing wrong — it followed the law, and the law says eat it. It is fresh, not a stock line: no cat appears, no one praises the taste of paper, and the dog never claims to be a good boy — it claims to be right.
